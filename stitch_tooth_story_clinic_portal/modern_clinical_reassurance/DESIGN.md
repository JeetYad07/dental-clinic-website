## Brand & Style
The design system articulates high-precision modern dentistry merged with an empathetic, calming patient experience. Designed for a progressive tech-corridor demographic, the visual language avoids intimidating sterile hospital tropes, instead delivering a tranquil, hospitable atmosphere that mitigates dental anxiety while signaling advanced clinical expertise.

The design movement combines **Contemporary Medical Modernism** with subtle **Tactile Precision**:
- **Pristine & Reassuring Whitespace:** Ample negative space creates a sense of breathability, order, and absolute hygienic clarity.
- **Deep Marine Stability:** Rich teal foundations convey uncompromising medical authority and composure.
- **Soft Bio-Luminescence:** Delicate mint accents, cyan-tinted fills, and fine micro-borders project vitality, fresh breath, and technological sophistication.
- **Approachable Humanism:** Smooth geometry, rounded badges, and friendly interactive cues dispel clinical coldness without diminishing professional rigor.

## Colors
The palette balances institutional trust, freshness, and immediate accessibility:

- **Primary (`#0A5C67` Deep Marine / `#0D7A88` Lighter Marine):** Anchors primary brand touchpoints, headers, vital interactive buttons, and trusted medical badging.
- **Secondary (`#10B981` Fresh Mint / `#34D399` Soft Emerald):** Communicates vitality, treatment success, healthy gum/oral hygiene metrics, and secondary supportive actions. A dedicated conversational green (`#25D366`) is reserved strictly for instant WhatsApp appointment booking touchpoints.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` Amber Gold):** Dedicated to trust validation metrics, Google Review ratings (4.9 stars), doctor credentials, and subtle highlight announcements.
- **Neutrals:**
  - **Neutral Dark (`#0F172A` Deep Slate / `#1E293B` Slate Body):** Provides high-contrast, fatigue-free typographic hierarchy.
  - **Neutral Light & Surfaces:** Base canvas spans clinical pure white (`#FFFFFF`), neutral sectioning off-white (`#F8FAFC`), and gentle cyan surface tints (`#F0FDFA`) for highlighted cards and patient notices.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans serves as the single typographic voice across all roles. Its geometric foundation provides clean clinical legibility, while subtly rounded apertures and terminals impart warmth and approachability.

- **Headlines:** Set with tight tracking (`-0.025em` to `-0.01em`) and assertive weights (700/800) to ensure clear narrative hierarchy and authoritative messaging without appearing clinical or rigid.
- **Body:** Calibrated at 16px and 18px with relaxed line heights (`1.5` to `1.55`) using `#1E293B` to maximize reading comfort for treatment descriptions and procedural FAQs.
- **Labels & Badges:** Slightly tracked out with medium-to-bold weights for rapid scanning on doctor credentials, treatment tags, and status chips.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to an 8px base modular cadence within a 12-column responsive grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a single-column layout on mobile devices.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max-width 1280px container, 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, and 32px (`2rem`) outer margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, and 24px (`1.5rem`) outer margins.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, and 16px (`1rem`) outer margins. Sticky bottom bar pattern reserved for direct WhatsApp and clinic calling.

Vertical spacing favors generous clinical breathing room. Section padding scales between 64px (`space-xl` * 1.6) on mobile to 96px - 120px on desktop to convey calm, deliberate organization.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation emphasizes clinical cleanliness, optical purity, and natural ambient light rather than dramatic drop-shadows:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Base Canvas:** `#F8FAFC` provides a tranquil backdrop.
  - **Surface Level 1 (Cards, Modules):** `#FFFFFF` with ultra-delicate borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
  - **Surface Tint (Highlights, Key Notes):** `#F0FDFA` paired with teal micro-borders (`1px solid #CCFBF1`).
- **Shadow Profiles:**
  - **Resting Elevation:** `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(10, 92, 103, 0.04), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)` creates weightless lift.
  - **Hover / Interactive Elevation:** `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(10, 92, 103, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)` provides responsive reassurance.
  - **Floating Booking Bar / Modals:** `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 0 1px rgba(10, 92, 103, 0.2)` ensures layered separation above content.

## Shapes
The design adopts a balanced **Rounded (Level 2)** shape discipline:
- Standard elements (inputs, doctor badges, cards) maintain a uniform 8px (`0.5rem`) corner radius.
- Larger diagnostic modules and treatment showcase panels scale up to 16px (`1rem`).
- Floating badges, status pills, and direct CTA actions (e.g., WhatsApp booking buttons) utilize complete pill encapsulation (`9999px`) to maintain tactile warmth, approachability, and ease of tap.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Clinical Action:** Solid `#0A5C67` surface, white text, 8px corner radius or full pill depending on context, height 48px, padded `1.5rem` horizontally. Smooth brightness transition on hover (`#0D7A88`).
- **Direct WhatsApp Booking Action:** Solid `#25D366` surface, white text, pill-shaped, paired with WhatsApp icon. Hover translates 1px upwards with a soft green shadow glow (`rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Outlined with `1.5px solid #0A5C67`, transparent fill, `#0A5C67` typography. On hover, fills with `#F0FDFA`.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- Crisp `#FFFFFF` background framed by a hairline border (`#E2E8F0`).
- Card padding set to `1.5rem` (`space-lg`).
- Service and specialist cards feature subtle top-border accent strips (2px in `#0D7A88` or `#10B981`) or delicate tinted icon backgrounds (`#F0FDFA`).

### Input Fields
- Height 48px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`.
- Active focus state: `1.5px solid #0A5C67` with an ambient focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(10, 92, 103, 0.12)`). Labels sit persistently above in `label-sm` slate.

### Chips & Trust Badges
- **Google Star Rating Badge:** `#FFFBEB` background, border `1px solid #FDE68A`, `#B45309` text, featuring a solid `#F59E0B` star icon.
- **Clinical Category Chip:** `#F0FDFA` background, `#0A5C67` text, 4px vertical / 12px horizontal padding, pill shape.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Rounded (4px for checkboxes, full circle for radios). Unchecked state: `#CBD5E1` border on pure white. Checked state: `#0A5C67` fill with crisp white internal mark.

### Specialized Dental Components
- **Before / After Comparison Slider:** Clean split-view container with a pill-shaped dual-arrow handle in `#0A5C67` and white indicator line.
- **Doctor Credential Card:** Features portrait with soft rounded mask (12px), title in deep marine, dental council registration badges in `#F8FAFC` tag blocks, and an embedded rating pill.
